Editor AI is provider-agnostic. Configure any supported backend in **Settings → Providers** and switch at any time without losing your sessions. Whether you want zero-cost community inference, a frontier cloud model, or fully private local generation, there is a supported provider for every setup.

## Provider Comparison

| Provider | Cost | Account Required | Setup Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Platinum** | Free | No | Minimal | Community Ollama proxy; volunteer-run servers by VLT GG |
| **Ollama** | Free | No | Low | Fully local; 6+ GB VRAM recommended |
| **Gemini** | Free tier / pay-as-you-go | Yes (Google account) | Low | Tight free-tier rate limits |
| **Claude** | Pay-as-you-go | Yes (Anthropic) | Low | Strong reasoning; vision supported |
| **OpenAI** | Pay-as-you-go | Yes (OpenAI) | Low | GPT-4o default; vision supported |
| **OpenRouter** | Free tier / pay-as-you-go | Yes (or browser sign-in) | Low | One key accesses many hosted models |
| **Mistral** | Pay-as-you-go | Yes (Mistral) | Low | European-hosted; good value |
| **HuggingFace** | Free tier / pay-as-you-go | Yes (or browser sign-in) | Low | Repo-style model IDs |
| **DeepSeek** | Pay-as-you-go | Yes (DeepSeek) | Low | Cost-effective; strong code/reasoning |
| **Groq** | Free tier / pay-as-you-go | Yes (Groq) | Low | Very fast inference; free tier available |
| **LM Studio** | Free | No | Medium | Local GUI app; OpenAI-compatible server |
| **llama.cpp** | Free | No | Medium | Lightweight local server; GGUF models |
| **Custom** | Varies | Varies | Medium | Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (BYOPAK) |

## Changing Providers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Editor AI panel">
    Press **E** anywhere in the game (desktop) or tap the floating **AI** bubble (mobile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Inside the panel, press **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Locate the **AI Provider** dropdown and select the backend you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the provider section">
    Scroll down to the matching provider section and fill in the required fields — API key, model name, or server URL depending on the provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your connection (optional)">
    Press **Test Connection** in the provider row to confirm everything is reachable before running a generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Test Connection** in the provider row to verify your key or server is reachable before generating. A failed test gives you an error message with enough detail to diagnose the problem early.
</Tip>

<Info>
  The **Subagent Provider** setting lets you route the internal consultant (`ask_subagent`) to a different provider — for example, directing it to **Platinum** for free background reasoning while using a paid model for main generation. Set it to `same` to mirror the main provider, or to `platinum` to always use the free community network for subagent calls regardless of your main provider.
</Info>

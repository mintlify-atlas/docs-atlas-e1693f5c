Editor AI is a [Geode](https://geode-sdk.org) mod for Geometry Dash that lets you describe any level you can imagine and have an AI build it directly in the editor. Blocks appear live as the AI works, sessions persist across restarts, and every generation lands on its own preview layer so you can accept, edit, or deny the result — your level, your call.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the mod">
    Download the latest `.geode` release from GitHub and install it through the Geode manual install button inside Geometry Dash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an AI provider">
    Open the Editor AI panel, go to **Settings**, and choose a provider. Two options — **Platinum** and **Ollama** — are completely free and need no account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your level">
    Head to the **Generate** tab, pick a target level, describe what you want, set difficulty/style/length, and press **Generate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept, edit, or deny">
    Watch blocks appear live in the editor. When generation finishes, the result sits on its own layer — review it, then choose to accept, continue editing, or deny.
  </Step>
</Steps>
